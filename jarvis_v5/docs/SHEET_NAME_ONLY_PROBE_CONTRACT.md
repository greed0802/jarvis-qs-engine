# Sheet-name-only Probe Contract

The future probe may only return sheet names after a separate approved build. Alpha36.1 keeps `sheet_name_probe_allowed=false`. The future output shape is contract-only and must not be interpreted as evidence that a workbook was checked.

Future success shape keeps workbook_content_read=false, formula_read=false, cell_value_read=false, and style_read=false.
