# Approval Escalation Contract

Approval levels are metadata-only in alpha36.0. Future approval labels do not grant runtime permission.

Levels:
- none_required
- user_acknowledgement
- metadata_permission_approval
- sheet_name_probe_approval_future
- content_read_approval_future
- output_generation_approval_future
- builder_preview_approval_future
- builder_export_approval_future

Future levels remain blocked until separate approved builds.
